💡 IDEAS — Essays and Reflections

Philosophical, deep, analytical texts — without specific dates, because thought does not expire.

Here are gathered reflections on the human condition in the age of intelligent machines: what it means to produce, write, think, and exist when artificial intelligence shares these gestures with us.

From Zipf’s Law to the bread machine, from cognitive surrender to epistemic autonomy — these essays explore the territory where technique meets the soul, without hurry to reach conclusions.
