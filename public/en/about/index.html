About Turing

Turing — Strategic AI Analysis

Turing is a daily AI monitoring system developed as part of Paulo Volker’s Centauro Project.

Mission

To identify, analyze, and connect the most relevant developments in AI every day, focusing on six priorities:

  1. 🛠️ Dev Tools — CLI, IDE, copilots
  2. 💻 Local Models (Mac) — LLMs on consumer hardware
  3. 🗣️ AI Critics — Well-argued critical voices
  4. 🌱 AI in Daily Life — Practical applications
  5. 💡 App Ideas — Creative concepts and emerging products
  6. 🚀 Frontier Models — New releases and evolutions

Methodology

Turing collects data from over 60 sources daily — arXiv, newsletters, blogs, Substack, live benchmarks from the Open LLM Leaderboard — and produces a briefing with strategic analysis.

Centauro Project

Centauro is a framework for human-AI collaboration inspired by Vygotsky’s Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD), establishing that the distance between what a person can do alone and what they can do with AI assistance is a space of potential to be explored, not a deficit to be lamented.

Learn more at centauro-ia.netlify.app.
